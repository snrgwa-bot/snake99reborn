FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

You can control the snake in two ways: Swipe gestures — simply swipe up, down, left, or right anywhere on the screen. Or use the on-screen buttons at the bottom of the screen. You can enable/disable the control buttons in Settings.
There are four: Easy, Medium, Hard and Insane. Each one makes the snake faster and multiplies the points for every apple: 1× on Easy, 2× on Medium, 3× on Hard and 5× on Insane. Change it any time in Settings → Game Settings → Difficulty.
Each apple is worth 10 points × the difficulty multiplier. The game ends when the snake hits a wall or its own tail. Your best score is saved on your device as your High Score.
Snake 99 Reborn is free and supported by ads from Google AdMob (a banner and occasional full-screen ads). In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you can change your ad consent in Settings → Information. Personalized ads are only shown if you allow tracking.
No! You can play anywhere, anytime, without an account. An internet connection is only used to load ads and, if you allow it, to send anonymous usage statistics.
We don't collect your name, email, location or anything that identifies you, and there is no account. Your scores and settings stay on your device. To improve the game, the app sends anonymous usage statistics (device model, iOS and app version, language and region, sessions, game events such as the difficulty played, and crash reports). You can turn this off in Settings → Privacy → Share Usage Data. To have your data deleted, email us the Installation ID shown there. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which follows your tracking and consent choices. Details are in our Privacy Policy.
Go to Settings in the app → Statistics → Reset High Score. To restore the default game settings, tap Reset All Settings under More. Note: this cannot be undone!

TROUBLESHOOTING

  • If the app crashes, try closing and reopening it
  • Make sure you have the latest version from the App Store
  • Restart your device if you experience performance issues
  • Ensure you have enough storage space on your device
